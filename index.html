About Us

Thread by thread, built on trust.

H&N Senile Embroidery is a Dhaka-based embroidery studio led by Managing Director Md. Hasan Ali. Since our beginnings, we have specialised in computerized embroidery that combines traditional craftsmanship with modern machine precision.

Whether it is a flat logo on a polo, a textured chenille letter on a varsity jacket, or shimmering sequin work, we take every stitch seriously. We work closely with our clients to make sure each design comes out clean, consistent and made to last.

What We Do

Types of embroidery we can do

From classic flat stitching to textured and decorative finishes.

🧵

Flat Embroidery

Clean, precise stitching for logos, names and graphics on fabric.

〰️

Chenille Embroidery

Soft, raised, velvet-like texture ideal for letters and patches.

✨

Sequin Embroidery

Shimmering sequin work that adds sparkle and dimension to any design.

🎚️

3D Puff Embroidery

Appliqué Work
